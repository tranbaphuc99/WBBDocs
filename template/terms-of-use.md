## What EveryHour is — and is not

EveryHour is a **documentation and estimation tool**. It helps you record the
hours you work, compares your pay stubs with those records using the wage
rules in the app's data, and prepares reports and letters from your records.

- **It is not legal advice**, and using it does not create a lawyer–client
  relationship. Wage laws change and have exceptions the app does not cover.
  For advice about your situation, contact a lawyer, a legal aid
  organization or the labor agency for your state.
- **Amounts and deadlines are estimates.** They depend on the records you
  enter, the numbers you confirm from your pay stubs, and the wage rules in
  the app at the time (each report shows the version of the rules and when
  they were last checked).
- **The app does not file anything** with an employer, agency or court, and
  does not contact anyone on your behalf.

## Your records

recover data that was deleted or lost with a phone.
