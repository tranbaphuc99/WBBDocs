# Framemate — Photo Coach — Support

Welcome to the support page for Framemate, the camera that coaches whoever is holding your phone.

If you experience any issues, have questions, or want to provide feedback, please contact us using the information below.

## Contact Support

**Email:** [vincenttrandeveloper@gmail.com](mailto:vincenttrandeveloper@gmail.com)

You can also send a bug report, feedback, or a feature suggestion directly from the app — open **Settings → Send feedback**. Reports sent this way include your app version and platform, which helps us diagnose issues faster. Only attach images you are comfortable sharing.

We aim to respond as quickly as possible.

## Common Support Topics

- Coaching instructions — distance, angle, framing, and when the shutter fires
- The burst taken at the moment of the shot, and reviewing which frames to keep
- Saving photos to your photo library and the **Your photos** screen
- Poses — saving one from your own photo, and pose search (Pro)
- **Learn my style** and the style profile built from your photos
- Live view on a second phone, and pairing with the four-digit code
- Voice cues and spoken instructions
- Subscription, purchase, or restore questions
- Permissions, storage, and deleting your data

## Coaching and the Shutter

Framemate reads each camera frame **on your phone** to work out the next instruction and the moment to shoot. If the coaching feels off:

1. The instructions are suggestions worked out from the camera picture — they will not always be right, and you can ignore any of them.
2. Make sure the Camera permission is granted — *Settings → Framemate*.
3. Poor light, a crowded background, or a subject too far from the camera all make the analysis less reliable. Move closer or find better light.
4. If voice cues are silent, check that your phone is not muted and that text-to-speech is available in your phone's accessibility settings.

## Photos, Poses, and Your Style

- **Photos you keep** are saved to your photo library in a "Framemate" album. On iPhone the app asks only for permission to *add* photos, so it never needs access to your whole library.
- **The Your photos screen** shows small thumbnails that the app keeps on your phone. They are not your originals — deleting a photo from your library does not remove the thumbnail, and **Settings → Delete all my data** removes the thumbnails.
- **Turning a photo into a pose** happens in the system photo picker, so the app only ever sees the photos you select.
- **Learn my style** builds statistics about how you are usually framed. It never keeps the photos it learns from.

## Pose Search (Pro)

Pose search looks through Unsplash for a pose to copy.

1. It is the one feature that needs an internet connection — everything else in Framemate works offline.
2. If you type in a language other than English, your phone translates the words before they are sent. On Android the translation model for your language downloads once, over Wi-Fi.
3. If search returns nothing, try fewer or simpler words, or tap one of the suggested scenes.
4. Photos come from Unsplash under the [Unsplash license](https://unsplash.com/license), and the app names the photographer of each one.

## Live View on a Second Phone

Live view sends a small, low-resolution preview from the phone taking the photo to one other phone you pair with a four-digit code.

1. The two phones talk **directly** — over Wi-Fi, a shared hotspot, or between two iPhones with no network at all. Nothing goes through the internet or through us.
2. If pairing fails, put both phones on the same Wi-Fi network or hotspot, keep them close together, and check the code.
3. Leaving the screen or turning live view off ends the connection. The second phone keeps nothing.

## Subscription & Billing

Framemate offers optional paid plans — **Framemate Pro** as a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase. Purchases are managed entirely through **Apple** or **Google**.

The yearly plan may start with a **7-day free trial**, offered by the store to customers new to the subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends and you will not be charged.

To restore a purchase on a new device or after reinstalling, open **Settings → Restore purchases** while signed in to the same Apple Account or Google account used for the original purchase.

For billing issues, cancellations, or refunds, use the store's own subscription management:

- **iOS:** Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions
- **Android:** Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions

## Your Data

Framemate keeps your poses, your style profile, your settings, your outing history, and the thumbnails of photos you kept **only on your phone**. We hold no copy and cannot restore anything for you.

**Settings → Delete all my data** erases all of it at once, along with any frames still waiting for review. Deleting the app does the same. Photos you already saved stay in your photo library — delete them there if you want them gone.

## Troubleshooting

Before contacting support, please try:

1. Restarting the app
2. Updating to the latest app version
3. Checking the Camera permission, and the photo permission if you use *Learn my style*
4. Restarting your device
5. Reinstalling the app if necessary — note that this **erases your poses, style profile, and thumbnails**, though photos already saved to your photo library are not affected
