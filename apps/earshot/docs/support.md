# Earshot — Voice to Text — Support

Welcome to the support page for Earshot — Voice to Text.

If you experience any issues, have questions, or want to provide feedback, please contact us using the information below.

**Email:** [vincenttrandeveloper@gmail.com](mailto:vincenttrandeveloper@gmail.com)

You can also send a bug report, feedback, or a feature suggestion directly from the app — open **Settings → Feedback**. Reports sent this way include your app version, which helps us diagnose issues faster.

We aim to respond as quickly as possible.

## Transcription & Speaker Identification

Earshot transcribes your recordings and identifies speakers **entirely on your iPhone** — nothing is sent to a server. If results seem off:

1. Background noise, overlapping speech, and distance from the microphone all affect accuracy — try recording closer to the speakers in a quieter space.
2. Speaker identification is a best-effort suggestion, not a certified match — you can rename or correct a speaker label at any time.
3. Make sure the Microphone and Speech Recognition permissions are granted — *Settings → Earshot*.
4. For longer or more accurate transcripts, try enabling the on-device AI model in **Settings**.

## On-Device AI Model (Pro)

The improved on-device AI option downloads one model file so summaries and answers can be generated without a network connection.

1. This requires an internet connection only for the initial download — after that, it runs fully offline.
2. If the download fails or stalls, check your connection and available storage, then try again from **Settings**.
3. Switching this off does not delete your existing transcripts or summaries.

## Subscription & Billing

Earshot offers optional paid plans (monthly, yearly, and lifetime). Purchases are managed entirely through **Apple** and the App Store.

To restore a purchase on a new device or after reinstalling, open **Settings → Restore Purchases** while signed in to the same Apple Account used for the original purchase.

For billing issues, cancellations, or refunds, use Apple's own subscription management:

- **iOS:** Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions

## Backing Up Your Data

Earshot stores your recordings, transcripts, and speaker data **only on your device** — there is no cloud sync by default and we hold no copy. Deleting the app or clearing its data removes everything permanently, and we cannot restore it for you.

If you want a copy kept somewhere else, use the in-app **export** feature, or turn on the optional **iCloud backup** (Pro) in Settings, which writes a copy into your own iCloud account.

## Troubleshooting

Before contacting support, please try:

1. Restarting the app
2. Updating to the latest app version
3. Restarting your device
4. Reinstalling the app if necessary — note that this **erases your local recordings and transcripts** unless you have exported them or enabled iCloud backup
