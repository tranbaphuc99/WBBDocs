## 2. What EveryHour is — and is not

EveryHour is a **documentation and estimation tool**. It helps you record the hours you work, compares your pay stubs against those records using the wage rules in the app's data, and prepares reports and letters from your records.

- **It is not legal advice**, and using it does not create a lawyer–client relationship. Wage laws change and have exceptions the app does not cover. For advice about your situation, contact a lawyer, a legal aid organization, or the labor agency for your state.
- **Amounts and deadlines are estimates.** They depend on the records you enter, the numbers you confirm from your pay stubs, and the wage rules in the app at the time — each report shows the version of those rules and when they were last checked.
- **Text read from a pay stub may be inaccurate or incomplete.** You are responsible for reviewing and correcting it before relying on it.
- **The App does not file anything** with an employer, agency, or court, and does not contact anyone on your behalf.

We may add, change, or suspend features without advance notice where permitted by law.

## 3. Your records — your responsibility

You are responsible for the accuracy of what you enter. The App keeps every change to a shift as a new entry with the reason you give, so your records show their history; please record your time honestly.

Your records are stored on your phone (see the [Privacy Policy](/WBBDocs/everyhour/privacy-policy)). **Keep your own backups** — we cannot recover data that was deleted, lost with a phone, or locked inside a backup whose password you no longer have.
