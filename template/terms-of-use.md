## What Framemate does

Framemate is a camera that coaches the person holding the phone — where to
stand, how to hold it and when to shoot — and helps you keep the best frames.
Its instructions are suggestions worked out by the phone from the camera
picture; they will not always be right, and a good photo still depends on the
light, the place and the people in it.

**Stay safe while you shoot.** Look where you are going. Do not step into
traffic, climb, lean over edges or go anywhere unsafe for a photo, whatever
the app suggests about distance or angle.
