Privacy Policy

Bundle ID: com.earshot.transcription.app
Platforms: iOS (App Store) only. Earshot does not ship on Android or Google Play.

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Summary — the short version​

Earshot is built so that what you record stays yours.

  • Your recordings and transcripts never leave your device. Speech-to-text, speaker identification, AI summaries, and search all run on your iPhone. There is no server that receives your audio, your transcripts, or anything derived from them (speaker labels, voice embeddings, summaries, search index).
  • There is no account. No sign-up, no email required, no password, no social login. Nothing ties the app to your identity.
  • No ads, no ad SDKs, no tracking across other apps or websites. Earshot does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt, because it has nothing to track you with.
  • We never sell your personal information.
  • What does leave the device: anonymous usage statistics and crash reports (to make the app work better), device-attestation tokens (to stop abuse of our servers), anonymous purchase/entitlement events (to unlock what you bought), and — only if you choose to use them — the content of a report you submit through the in-app feedback form, an optional AI model file you choose to download, or an optional iCloud backup you turn on yourself.

The rest of this policy explains each of those points precisely.

1. Scope​

This policy applies to the Earshot app distributed on the Apple App Store, and to any future updates, to the extent that the features described are actually enabled on your device. Earshot is iOS-only; there is no Android or Google Play version of this policy to reconcile.

It does not cover Apple's own processing of your data as the platform and store operator — including iCloud, if you choose to use the optional backup feature described in section 3.8 — which is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

3. Data processed off the device​

3.1 Usage analytics — Google Firebase Analytics​

Earshot records anonymous, aggregated usage events so we can see which features are used and where people get stuck. Examples of what is recorded:

  • which screen you are viewing (Record, Library, Session Detail, Settings, Feedback, Paywall);
  • that a recording started, finished, or failed, and roughly how long it ran — never its content;
  • that speaker identification, a summary, or a search ran, and which engine produced it (the built-in free method, or the optional on-device AI model) — never the transcript, the summary text, the speaker names, or the search query itself;
  • that the paywall was opened, a plan was selected, and whether a purchase started, completed, failed, or was cancelled — and which plan tier, never payment details;
  • that "restore purchases" was tapped;
  • that the app's rating prompt was shown;
  • that a feedback report was submitted, and how many images were attached.

What is never sent as analytics: the audio itself, the text of your transcripts, summaries, speaker names, tags, or search queries. Event values are limited in length and stripped to plain identifiers before being sent.

Firebase assigns an app-instance ID to your installation and receives basic technical context (platform, OS version, app version, device model, coarse region/language, IP address at the time of the request). Earshot is configured without advertising-identifier support, so no IDFA is collected and this data is not used for advertising or cross-app tracking. The app-instance ID is reset if you delete and reinstall the app, or clear app data.

Processor: Google LLC / Firebase — see the Firebase privacy documentation.

3.2 Crash reports — Google Firebase Crashlytics​

If the app crashes or hits a fatal error, a crash report is sent so we can fix it. A report contains the stack trace, the app version, the device model, the OS version, and technical state at the moment of the crash. It does not contain your recordings, transcripts, or any of their content, and it is not used to identify you.

Processor: Google LLC / Firebase.

3.3 Abuse prevention — Google Firebase App Check​

Before Earshot talks to our Firebase services, it asks the operating system to prove the app is a genuine, untampered install (App Attest / DeviceCheck). The result is a short-lived attestation token. This protects our backend from spam and abuse. The token attests the app, not you — it carries no personal data and cannot be used to identify you.

Processor: Apple Inc. (App Attest / DeviceCheck) and Google LLC (Firebase App Check).

3.4 Remote configuration​

Earshot reads a small, public, read-only configuration document from Cloud Firestore at startup, gated by App Check with no sign-in of any kind. It tells the app things like whether the feedback form is currently accepting submissions, and which on-device AI model options are currently offered. This is a read only — nothing about you is sent. As with any network request, Google receives your IP address and basic technical data to serve the response.

3.5 The in-app feedback form (bug report / feedback / feature suggestion)​

This is the only place in normal use where you deliberately send us content. It is entirely optional — the app is fully usable without ever opening it.

When you submit a report, the following is stored in Cloud Firestore and Firebase Storage:

  • the email address you enter (so we can reply — you choose whether to give a real one);
  • the report type (bug report, feedback, or feature suggestion);
  • your message (up to 2,000 characters);
  • up to 3 images you attach (max 5 MB each);
  • the anonymous ID described in section 2, the app version, and a server timestamp.

Your recordings and transcripts are never attached automatically. If you choose to describe a problem by attaching a screenshot, only the image you explicitly pick is uploaded — the photo picker never gives Earshot access to your photo library as a whole, and Earshot does not read location or other metadata from the images you attach.

Retention. Reports are kept while we work through them and for a reasonable period afterwards as a record of what was fixed and why. You can ask us to delete your report and its attachments at any time — see section 9.

3.6 Optional on-device AI model download (Pro)​

If you turn on the improved on-device AI option in Settings, Earshot downloads one AI model file over HTTPS from a third-party file-hosting service, Hugging Face, and stores it on your device. This is a plain file download: your device's IP address and standard request metadata reach Hugging Face's servers, exactly as with any file download from any website, but no recording, transcript, or other content of yours is sent as part of this download — the traffic only pulls a generic, publicly published model file, it never uploads anything. Once downloaded, the model runs entirely on your device; nothing about how you use it is sent anywhere.

Processor: Hugging Face, Inc. — file delivery only, no personal data exchanged.

3.7 Purchases and subscriptions — RevenueCat and Apple​

Earshot offers optional paid plans (monthly, yearly, and a one-time lifetime purchase). Payment itself is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store on your device.

  • We never see or receive your card number, bank details, or billing address.
  • Earshot uses RevenueCat to determine which plan is active and unlock the right features. RevenueCat receives an anonymous, RevenueCat-generated identifier for your install (Earshot does not log this identifier in with any account, because there is no account), which App Store product you purchased, whether it is active, and basic device/platform information. This entitlement state is also cached locally so the app knows what to unlock while offline.
  • "Restore purchases" asks Apple and RevenueCat to re-report purchases already tied to your Apple Account.
  • Managing or cancelling a subscription opens Apple's own subscription management screen.

Processors: RevenueCat, Inc. (entitlement management) and Apple Inc. (App Store billing). Apple's handling of your purchase data is governed by Apple's privacy policy, not this one; RevenueCat's is governed by RevenueCat's privacy policy.

3.8 Optional iCloud backup (Pro)​

If you turn on iCloud backup in Settings, Earshot writes a copy of your session library — transcripts, speaker data, and the audio files — into a private container inside your own iCloud account, so a lost or new phone does not mean a lost library. This is:

  • off by default — nothing is backed up unless you turn it on;
  • one-way, restore-on-demand — Earshot writes files there and can read them back to restore a library, but this is not a live, continuous, multi-device sync;
  • stored under your Apple ID's iCloud storage, not on a server we operate. We never receive a copy of it. Apple's handling of the data in your iCloud account is governed by Apple's own privacy policy, and it counts against your iCloud storage quota.

3.9 Notifications and Live Activity​

Earshot does not send push notifications and has no push infrastructure — it registers no push token and cannot message you from our side. While recording, the app can show a Live Activity (Lock Screen / Dynamic Island) with the current recording duration (Pro); this is drawn locally by the app on your device and involves no server or notification service.

3.10 Rating prompt​

Earshot may ask the operating system to display the native App Store rating dialog. The dialog, and any review you write in it, are handled by Apple. We only record internally that the prompt was shown (see section 3.1); we never see whether or how you rated the app.

5. How we use the data​

We process the limited data described above to:

  • provide recording, transcription, speaker identification, summaries, search, and export;
  • keep the app stable — diagnosing crashes and errors;
  • understand which features are used, so we know what to improve;
  • unlock paid features you bought, via Apple and RevenueCat;
  • reply to a feedback report you sent us, and fix what it describes;
  • protect our services from abuse and comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, automated decision-making, or cross-app or cross-site tracking. We do not sell personal information, and we do not "share" it for cross-context behavioural advertising as those terms are used in California law.

9. Your rights​

Depending on where you live — including under the GDPR/UK GDPR, the CCPA/CPRA in California, and comparable laws elsewhere — you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal information, to receive a copy of it in a portable format, to withdraw consent, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

In practice, for Earshot:

  • Data on your device — you control it directly. Delete individual recordings in the app, clear the app's data in system settings, or uninstall the app. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete.
  • Analytics and crash data — reinstalling the app, or clearing app data, resets the app-instance ID and disconnects future data from past data. To have data associated with your current installation deleted, email us; we will need the approximate dates so we can locate it.
  • Purchase/entitlement data at RevenueCat — email us and we will submit a deletion request on your behalf, or contact RevenueCat directly.
  • A feedback report you sent us — email us from the address you used, or quote the report, and we will delete the report and its attachments.
  • iCloud backup — delete it yourself from Settings, or from your iCloud storage settings; we never hold a copy.

Write to vincenttrandeveloper@gmail.com. We aim to respond within 30 days. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority; in the EEA, that is the authority for your country of residence.

10. Children​

Earshot is a general-audience utility app. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher minimum age set by your local law, such as 16 in parts of the EEA), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app contains no ads, no social features, no user-to-user content, and no external links other than to the App Store.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has sent us personal information through the feedback form, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Recording other people​

Earshot lets you record audio you capture with your device's microphone, including conversations with other people. You are responsible for complying with the law that applies to you — including any requirement to obtain the other participants' consent before recording a conversation, which varies by country, and in the United States, by state. We do not monitor, moderate, or have access to what you record, and we cannot advise you on the recording-consent law in your specific situation.

12. Security​

Data on your device is protected by the operating system's app sandbox and device encryption. Data in transit is sent over HTTPS/TLS. Access to our Firebase project is restricted to the publisher, requests are attested with App Check, and Firestore security rules limit what the app can read and write.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

13. Changes to this policy​

We may update this policy as the app changes. The "Last updated" date at the top will change, and for material changes — for example a genuinely new category of data collection — we will give notice in the app or on the store listing before the change takes effect. Continuing to use the app after a change takes effect means the updated policy applies.
