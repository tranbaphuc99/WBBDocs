## 2. What Framemate does

Framemate is a camera that coaches the person holding the phone — where to stand, how to hold it, and when to shoot — and helps you keep the best frames.

- **Its instructions are suggestions**, worked out by the phone from the camera picture. They will not always be right, and a good photo still depends on the light, the place, and the people in it.
- **Pose search, live view, and the other Pro features** may be added, changed, or suspended without advance notice where permitted by law.

### 2.1 Stay safe while you shoot

Live view sends a live preview from the phone taking the photo to one other phone you pair with a four-digit code, directly between the two devices. Only start it with a phone you control or trust, and be aware that whoever holds the second phone can see what the camera sees while it is running.
